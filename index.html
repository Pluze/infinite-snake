Keep growing in an endless world.

Each food adds 10 points and one body segment. Choose a world size when starting a new game. With Wraparound on, left joins right and top joins bottom. Repeated regions show the same board. Turn Wraparound off when starting a new game to play on one board with solid walls. Hitting your own body or a wall ends the game. Filling the entire world wins.

Play manually by default. One food appears at a time. Eating it spawns another on a free cell. Optional autoplay looks for short, safe food routes across the seams. It falls back to a winding route when a shortcut would leave gaps or block continued growth. After manual play, autoplay checks tail access while recovering its coverage route. Pause before risk is an optional assist.
